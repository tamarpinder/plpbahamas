# PLP Mobile Application - User Guide

**Progressive Liberal Party Mobile Application**  
**User Guide and Quick Start Manual**  
**Version 1.0 - June 2025**

---

## Welcome to the PLP Mobile App

The Progressive Liberal Party Mobile Application connects Bahamian citizens with their political representatives and community activities. This user-friendly platform provides access to party news, upcoming events, live streams, and opportunities for civic engagement throughout The Bahamas.

## Quick Start Guide

### Accessing the Application

1. **Open your web browser** on your mobile device or computer
2. **Navigate to the application URL** provided by your PLP representative
3. **Choose your access method:**
   - **Sign Up** - Create a new account for full features
   - **Log In** - Access your existing account
   - **Continue as Guest** - Browse without registration

### Creating Your Account

**Step 1: Registration Information**
- Enter your full name as it appears on official documents
- Provide a valid email address for account verification
- Add your phone number for important notifications
- Select your voting district from the dropdown menu

**Step 2: Personal Preferences**
- Choose your age range and gender (optional)
- Select areas of interest (Healthcare, Education, Economy, etc.)
- Set notification preferences for news and events

**Step 3: Account Activation**
- Review your information for accuracy
- Accept the terms of service and privacy policy
- Complete registration to access your personalized dashboard

## Main Features Overview

### Dashboard - Your Political Hub
The dashboard displays key information at a glance:
- **Active Members**: Current party membership statistics
- **Latest News**: Recent political announcements and updates
- **Upcoming Events**: Next scheduled rallies, town halls, and meetings
- **Quick Actions**: Easy access to news, events, and live streams

### News Feed - Stay Informed
Access comprehensive political news and party announcements:
- **Read Articles**: Tap any news item to read the full story
- **Engage with Content**: Like, comment, and share articles
- **Filter by Category**: Find news about Healthcare, Community, Employment
- **Track Engagement**: See how many people have interacted with each story

### Events Calendar - Get Involved
Discover and participate in political activities:
- **Browse Events**: View all upcoming rallies, forums, and town halls
- **Event Details**: See dates, times, locations, and descriptions
- **RSVP System**: Confirm attendance or express interest
- **Live Streaming**: Join virtual events when available

### Live Streams - Watch in Real-Time
Participate in live political events from anywhere:
- **Active Streams**: Watch ongoing events and announcements
- **Upcoming Schedule**: See when future streams are planned
- **Interactive Features**: Participate in live discussions
- **Notifications**: Get alerts when important streams begin

### Profile Management - Customize Your Experience
Manage your account and preferences:
- **Update Information**: Change contact details and preferences
- **Notification Settings**: Control what alerts you receive
- **Activity History**: Review your RSVPs and interactions
- **Privacy Controls**: Manage your data sharing preferences

## Using the Mobile Interface

### Navigation
The bottom navigation bar provides quick access to all sections:
- **Home** (🏠): Return to your dashboard
- **News** (📰): Browse political news and updates
- **Events** (📅): View and RSVP to upcoming events
- **Live** (📺): Access live streams and broadcasts
- **Profile** (👤): Manage your account settings

### Touch Interactions
- **Tap**: Select items, open articles, navigate between sections
- **Scroll**: Browse through news articles and event listings
- **Swipe**: Navigate between different content sections
- **Long Press**: Access additional options (where available)

## Participating in Events

### Finding Events
1. **Tap the Events tab** in the bottom navigation
2. **Browse the event list** showing upcoming activities
3. **Read event details** including date, time, and location
4. **Check for live streaming** availability (marked with 🔴)

### RSVP Process
1. **Select an event** you want to attend
2. **Choose your response:**
   - **RSVP**: Confirm you will attend
   - **Interested**: Express interest without firm commitment
3. **Receive confirmation** of your response
4. **Get reminders** as the event approaches

### Virtual Participation
- **Live Streamed Events** allow remote participation
- **Receive notifications** when streams begin
- **Join from anywhere** with internet access
- **Participate in discussions** during live events

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Login Problems
- **Forgot Password**: Use the password reset option on the login screen
- **Account Locked**: Contact support if you cannot access your account
- **Email Issues**: Check spam folder for verification emails

### Technical Issues
- **App Not Loading**: Check your internet connection and refresh the page
- **Slow Performance**: Close other browser tabs and restart the app
- **Display Problems**: Try rotating your device or adjusting text size

### Content Issues
- **Missing Events**: Ensure you've selected the correct voting district
- **No Notifications**: Check your notification preferences in Profile settings
- **Outdated Information**: Pull down to refresh content in any section

## Getting Help
